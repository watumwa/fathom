# Fathom Professional Photography System

## Direction
Fathom's photography should feel documentary, credible and East African: natural light, real work, real agribusiness environments, restrained earthy colour, and people actively making decisions. Avoid generic corporate handshakes, overly staged stock poses, saturated filters, fake text/logos, and unrelated coffee-shop imagery.

## Image library and purpose
- `photography/coffee-harvest.webp` — farm planning, production, farmer/farm-investor stories.
- `photography/field-advisory.webp` — Fathom's hands-on advisory proposition and field consulting.
- `photography/farmer-training.webp` — capacity building, cooperatives, producer groups and programmes.
- `photography/coffee-sorting.webp` — processing, quality, post-harvest and value-chain insights.
- `photography/business-advisory.webp` — business plans, financing readiness, SMEs and contact/conversion areas.
- `photography/product-branding.webp` — product development, branding, market positioning and go-to-market.
- `photography/commodity-warehouse.webp` — commodity trade, logistics, quality/volume and commercial operations.
- `photography/coffee-landscape.webp` — high-level brand storytelling and section/page hero imagery.

## Page treatment
- Home: harvesting hero for immediate Uganda coffee context.
- About: landscape hero + field advisory + farm/product/processing visual story.
- Services: landscape introduction; every service has its own relevant image.
- Service detail pages: each hero inherits the service-specific photography.
- Who We Serve: training/community hero with harvesting and SME/business imagery.
- Insights: processing/quality hero, field feature, and unique farm/product/trade cards.
- Contact: business advisory image to shift the tone from agriculture into a professional engagement.
- 404: landscape image to keep even utility pages on-brand.

## Production replacement policy
The generated images are art-directed placeholders suitable for the current presentation. As Fathom builds its own photo archive, replace them one-for-one with commissioned photographs using the same subjects/crops. Preserve filenames to avoid code changes.

## Recommended real Fathom shoot list
1. Founder/advisor portrait in a coffee field.
2. Advisor and grower inspecting a coffee plant.
3. Wide Ugandan coffee farm landscape.
4. Selective harvesting of ripe red cherries.
5. Farmer/cooperative training session.
6. Coffee drying/sorting/quality-control process.
7. SME owner reviewing packaged coffee/product samples.
8. Coffee cupping or product evaluation.
9. Business planning meeting with an SME client.
10. Warehouse/bags/commodity quality inspection.
11. Team/founder professional environmental portraits.
12. Client success/case-study portraits with permission.

## Technical standard
Use WebP/AVIF where possible. Hero source images should ideally be at least 1920px wide; card images at least 1200px wide. Keep important faces/actions away from extreme edges so responsive `object-fit: cover` crops remain safe. Aim for <250 KB for normal cards and <450 KB for hero images after compression.
